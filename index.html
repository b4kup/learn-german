A series of three · A1 to C2

Contrastive Grammar Courses

German, Danish and English — each written for a speaker of one of the others

Three self-contained grammar courses built on the same method: explicit rules anchored in examples, quizzes as retrieval practice, and a systematic comparison with the learner’s native language so that positive transfer is used and negative transfer is flagged before it becomes a habit. All files work offline; progress and quiz scores are saved in your browser.

Volume 1

German Grammar for Danish Speakers

Tysk grammatik for dansktalende

Four cases, three genders, adjective declension, verb-final subordinate clauses. Danish parallels in red.
Volume 2

Danish Grammar for German Speakers

Dänische Grammatik für Deutschsprachige

Stød and soft d, the suffixed article, sin/sit/sine, adverb placement, the s-passive. German parallels in blue.
Volume 3

English Grammar for German Speakers

Englische Grammatik für Deutschsprachige

Present Perfect vs. Perfekt, Verlaufsform, do-Umschreibung, kein V2, Wortstellung, Artikel, falsche Freunde. Erklärtexte auf Deutsch.

How to read the colour code

BlueGerman — the target language in Volume 1, the parallel language (“Deutsche Parallele”) in Volumes 2 and 3.
RedDanish — the parallel language (“Dansk parallel”) in Volume 1, the target language in Volume 2.
GreenEnglish — the target language in Volume 3.
AmberTrap boxes — the exact spot where a habit from the native language produces an error in the target language.

Common structure

  • 30 chapters in three parts — Foundations (A1), Core grammar (A2–B1), Advanced (B2–C2) — each tagged with the CEFR level at which the structure is normally introduced.
  • Quiz + exercise per chapter. Quizzes are interactive with shuffled options and instant feedback; exercises have hidden solutions. Scores are saved locally.
  • Sidebar with chapter filter, progress bar and scroll-tracking; “Mark chapter as done” in every chapter.
  • Appendices: sound-shift cheat sheet, false friends, cognates or collocations, a staged study plan with benchmarks, and caveats.
  • Print-ready: printing (or saving as PDF) expands all quizzes, exercises and answer keys and hides the navigation.
Methodological basis across all volumes: contrastive analysis (Lado), focus-on-form (Long), retrieval practice (Roediger & Karpicke), the spacing effect, and CEFR grammar profiles for level tagging.
